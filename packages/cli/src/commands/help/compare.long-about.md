Extracts two revisions with one projection definition, runs its comparator and
presents how one became the other: as accessible text, the saved comparison
document (--json) or an image (--png).\
\<BASE>..\<HEAD> compares two revisions; \<BASE>...\<HEAD> compares from their
merge base, as a pull request does; one revision compares with the working tree.
An empty side means HEAD.
